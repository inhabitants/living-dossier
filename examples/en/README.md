# Example: a fictional patient

**Every piece of data in this folder is made up.** It only shows the shape of what Living Dossier produces. No value, person or family here is real.

- [00_Hub.md](00_Hub.md): the dashboard
- [Correlator.md](Correlator.md): the crossings by axis, with the three tags

**Fictional profile:** woman, 46, lives in Lisbon. Lab results from 2019 to 2025, a consumer genotyping chip, a mother with type 2 diabetes at 52, a father with high blood pressure since 60, a blood pressure monitor at home.

A Portuguese version lives in [`../pt-BR/`](../pt-BR/).
