# Disclaimer

Living Dossier is a tool for **organizing, synthesizing and structuring personal health information**. It was born from a personal experience of self-knowledge and is shared in that spirit.

- **It is not a medical device.** It does not diagnose, predict, prevent or treat any condition.
- **Your doctor has the final word** on any decision about your health.
- **Medication only with a prescription and medical follow-up.** Do not start, stop or change medication based on what the dossier shows. The dossier turns those topics into questions for your appointment.
- **Genetics indicates predisposition.** A risk variant is something to discuss with your doctor. High-impact findings call for confirmation in a clinical lab and genetic counseling.
- **AI makes mistakes.** Reading photos, extracting PDFs and interpreting them can go wrong. Always check the value against the original report.
- **Health is individual.** What served the author is a starting point, never a rule.
- **Emergency:** call **112** (Europe), **911** (US) or your local emergency number. An acute symptom needs immediate care, not a dossier.
